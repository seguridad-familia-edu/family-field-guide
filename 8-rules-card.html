OUR FAMILY RULES

If a call, text, or post involves money, trouble, codes, or anger — run the list. Tape this near the phone.

1Any call about money, bail, codes, or trouble: hang up. Call back on the number you already have. (On the back of your card, your statement — never the number that called you.)
2Family member "in trouble" on the phone? Ask the safe word. No safe word — no exceptions, no matter how real the voice sounds. Hang up and call them yourself.
3Never read, forward, or type a text-message code for anyone. That code is the key to your account. Nobody real ever needs it.
4Gift cards are gifts, not payments. Anyone who asks to be paid in gift cards — bank, IRS, court, "bail," "utility" — is a criminal. Full stop.
5Story or video that makes you furious or terrified? Don't forward it yet. Check it — or send it to family first and ask.

Scams run on secrecy and speed. Talking and checking delete both. If it happens anyway: call the bank first (fast), then report at reportfraud.ftc.gov and ic3.gov — and tell the family the same day. The only one shame protects is the scammer.
